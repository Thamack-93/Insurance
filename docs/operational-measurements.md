# Operational measurements

After metric corrections ship, record a weekly baseline and the same definitions
for every later report:

- overdue collection follow-ups and promise fulfillment by promised business date;
- overdue claim requirements among outstanding requirements with explicit deadlines;
- unresolved commission discrepancies grouped by currency;
- reviewed quote comparisons and recorded selections.

Each report includes the date range, denominator, organization scope, and data
availability. Missing historical contact events are not reconstructed. Superseded
promises remain history and are excluded from the active promise denominator.
